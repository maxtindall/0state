frankcoin
introduction

0state is a political organization governed by the miners of frankcoin. Membership is established through proof-of-work rather than purchase: franks serve as a currency, but the right to participate in governance is distinct from the right to hold them.

governance

franks are a currency — they may be earned, held, and freely traded. Governance rights are held separately. The franchise is acquired only through mining and is non-transferable, so no accumulation of capital can acquire control of the organization. Each member holds one vote; voting weight reflects recent mining activity and diminishes with inactivity. Proposals are decided by simple plurality: the option with the most votes prevails.

membership

Membership is open and permissionless. Mining a single frankcoin proof confers it — there is no application and no admitting authority. It is contingent on continued participation; a member who ceases to mine gradually forfeits standing. The reference miner is available at frankcoin.website.

taking part

Membership and voting live in the terminal, where each member signs with their own mining wallet. The whole path, from nothing to a cast vote:

# 1 — install the miner (macOS) $ brew install maxtindall/frankcoin/frankcoin # 2 — mine; one accepted proof qualifies you $ frankmine # 3 — install the client, then become a member $ brew install maxtindall/frankcoin/0state $ 0state join # 4 — take part $ 0state status # the organization, and your standing $ 0state proposals # open questions and their tallies $ 0state propose "<title>" --body "…" $ 0state vote <id> yes|no|abstain

Signing happens locally with your own Solana keypair; nothing is custodied.

0state D.A.O. is currently live on Solana's devnet. Its franks are a test network's and are worth nothing. Nothing here is an offer, a sale, a security, or financial advice.
