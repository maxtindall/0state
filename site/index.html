0state is CityDAO, on Solana — a commune run as an artwork whose citizenship is a freely transferable Citizen NFT: mint one, hold it, and holding it is your membership and your vote (one NFT, one vote); the vote travels with the token when it is bought, sold, or traded, exactly as CityDAO's Citizen NFTs did, while a UNA → Wyoming DAO LLC wrapper lets the commune hold real-world assets as its citizens direct, and STATE — mined, spendable from the commons treasury only by a passed vote — is the currency, no longer the franchise. Mint a citizen and take part from the terminal: brew install maxtindall/frankcoin/0state, then 0state mint. Full source: github.com/maxtindall/0state.

Live on Solana's devnet. Its Citizen NFTs and STATE are a test network's and are worth nothing. Nothing here is an offer, a sale, a security, or financial advice.
