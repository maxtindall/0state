Summary

The two chain projects, once a single coupled pair, are now independent. 0state has cut loose from frankcoin and mines its own franchise, the STATE token. frankcoin has left the commune to become a standalone memecoin — mined and freely traded — led by the General Secretary. All three on-chain programs were built, deployed or upgraded, and verified on devnet.

What changed

  • 0state → a communist CityDAO. A new proof-of-work program mints STATE. Mining STATE is the sole, un-buyable qualification for citizenship; one STATE in ten is routed to a keyless commons treasury, spent only by a passed vote. No steward, no admin — fully autonomous.
  • frankcoin → a memecoin under the General Secretary. The 10% levy and the 0state coupling were removed; every mined frank now goes to the miner. Democratic centralism, enforced in bytecode — the Party is the code.
  • zerostate (the voting program) now reads STATE proofs for membership and vote-weight, not frankcoin's.

On the money

STATE and frankcoin are both uncapped, on a Dogecoin-style model: the reward halves across a distribution phase, then floors at a perpetual 1-per-proof tail — emission never stops, and percentage inflation trends to zero. frankcoin's franks are SPL tokens with no freeze authority and a keyless mint authority; no program change can touch, freeze, or seize them.
